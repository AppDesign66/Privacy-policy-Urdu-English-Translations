1. Information We Collect and Process

Our core mission is to provide you with seamless translation and language learning without compromising your privacy. We do not require you to register an account, sign in, or provide personal contact details (such as your legal name, physical address, or phone number) to use the Application.

A. User-Provided Information

  • Translation & Dictionary Queries: Any text or vocabulary you type or search within the dictionary is processed locally or in-flight to return your translation/definition. We do not store, share, or sell your personal search logs on any remote private database.
  • Local History & Favorites: The app saves your translation history and bookmarked words strictly locally on your device (using an on-device encrypted Room SQLite database). This data never leaves your device and can be permanently deleted by you at any time using the "Clear History" button within the app settings.

  • Diagnostic & Performance Data: Application crash logs, ANR (Application Not Responding) events, and memory status to resolve technical issues.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

The Application requests only the minimal permissions strictly necessary for its functionality:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (Normal Permissions): Required to fetch online translations for 60+ languages, check network status, and display ads from Google AdMob.
